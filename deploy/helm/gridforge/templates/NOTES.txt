GridForge is deployed as {{ include "gridforge.fullname" . }}.

The service is cluster-internal on port {{ .Values.service.port }}.
Forward it locally with:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "gridforge.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Or from a checkout of this repository:

  NAMESPACE={{ .Release.Namespace }} RELEASE={{ include "gridforge.fullname" . }} just port-forward {{ .Values.service.port }}

Then open http://localhost:8080/.
